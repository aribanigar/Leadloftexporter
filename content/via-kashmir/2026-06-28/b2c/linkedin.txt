Kashmir is open and at its easy best this week. Srinagar sits in the low twenties, the Mughal gardens are in full colour, and a shikara at dawn on the Dal is the calmest, most beautiful hour the city gives you. It is the warm, gentle window before the early-July rush. If you have been waiting for the right time to see the valley, this is it - plan it with people who live here at viakashmir.in.
