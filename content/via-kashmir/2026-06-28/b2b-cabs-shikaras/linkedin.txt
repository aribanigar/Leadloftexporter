Every pass in Kashmir is open right now - Sonamarg, Gulmarg, Pahalgam - and travellers are booking full-day valley drives and golden-hour shikaras as fast as they can. An empty seat on a clear-road day never comes back; the traveller who needed that drive simply books the next driver they can find. If you run cabs or shikaras, the fix is to be findable: list your service on viakashmir.in and let riders come to you. contact@viakashmir.in
