This is the busiest the valley gets, and travellers are booking rides all day - airport pickups, full-day drives to Sonamarg and Pahalgam, a shikara at golden hour. The operators travellers can find are the ones filling their week; the empty seat and the idle boat at peak season simply do not come back. Listing your cab or shikara on ViaKashmir puts you in front of travellers at the exact moment they are planning, so fewer days go to waste and the traveller books you direct. It takes minutes. List your service at viakashmir.in/sign-up or write to contact@viakashmir.in.
