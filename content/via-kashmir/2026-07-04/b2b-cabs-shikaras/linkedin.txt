Kashmir is in peak season and travellers are booking rides every day - airport pickups, full-day valley drives, a shikara at golden hour. The operators travellers can find are the ones getting booked; the empty seat and the idle boat simply do not come back. Listing your cab or shikara on ViaKashmir puts you in front of travellers at the exact moment they are planning, so fewer days go to waste, and the traveller books you direct. It takes minutes. List your service at viakashmir.in/sign-up or write to contact@viakashmir.in.
