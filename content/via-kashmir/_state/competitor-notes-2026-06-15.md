# Competitor product notes — week of 2026-06-15

Study of how large travel marketplaces PRODUCTS behave (not how they talk). Internal
only — never name any of these in ViaKashmir output.

## What they show
- **MakeMyTrip / Goibibo**: hero is a single large destination/season image with a very
  short headline and one filled primary CTA above the fold. Product density is low up
  top (one offer per screen), then dense lists below. Urgency is soft and seasonal
  ("monsoon escapes", "summer in the hills"), not countdown timers.
- **Booking.com**: search-first; the "product" is the listing card — photo, name, one
  trust line (free cancellation / pay at property), and a single action. Strong
  loss-aversion microcopy ("In high demand", "Only 2 left", "Booked 14 times today").
- **IndiGo (goindigo.in)**: clean, one dominant CTA, minimal colour, fare hooks we
  deliberately ignore (no prices for us).

## What we should copy this week
- One photo, one headline, one filled CTA above the fold — exactly our hero+hook unit.
- Trust line directly under the CTA (we use the reassurance line: "Open all season",
  "No cost to list, your guests"). Safety/openness perception is the real B2C barrier.
- For B2B, borrow the listing-demand urgency device ("in demand right now / booked
  today") rephrased as our loss-aversion hook ("that booking just went to another
  hotel") — show the demand, never a sales pitch, never commission.

## One thing to avoid
- Countdown timers, fake scarcity numbers, and price/discount framing. We sell by
  SHOWING Kashmir (big photo, confident short copy), with no numbers anywhere.
