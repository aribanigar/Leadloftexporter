Late June might be the easiest time all year to see Kashmir. Srinagar sits in the low twenties, every pass to Gulmarg, Sonamarg and Pahalgam is open, and the gondola is running over green meadows to snow that is still on the peaks - all while much of the country swelters. We plan the whole trip end to end with people who actually live in the valley, so travellers simply land and enjoy it. If a cool-weather escape has been on your list, this is the window, just before the July crowds. Plan yours at viakashmir.in.
