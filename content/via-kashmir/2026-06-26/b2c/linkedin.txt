Kashmir is open and at its easy best this week. Srinagar is in the low twenties, every pass to Sonamarg, Gulmarg and Pahalgam is clear, and the high glaciers are still bright above the green summer meadows. It is the gentle window before the July rush. If you have been waiting for the right time to see the valley, this is it - plan it with people who live here at viakashmir.in.
