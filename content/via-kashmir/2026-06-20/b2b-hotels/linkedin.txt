Peak summer is here and travellers are booking Kashmir stays every day - the question for hotel owners is simply whether they can find you. Every day a property is not listed, a room next door gets the guest instead. Listing on viakashmir.in puts your rooms in front of travellers actively planning their trip, and the booking relationship stays yours. If you run a hotel or guesthouse in the valley, list it at viakashmir.in/sign-up?role=vendor or write to contact@viakashmir.in.
