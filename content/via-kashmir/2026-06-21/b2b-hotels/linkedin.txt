It is peak season in Kashmir and the valley is filling fast - travellers are booking stays every day and accommodation is going quickly. The only question for a hotel owner is whether they can find you when they search. Every day a property is not listed, the room next door gets the guest instead. Listing on viakashmir.in puts your rooms in front of travellers actively planning their trip, and the booking relationship stays yours. If you run a hotel or guesthouse in the valley, list it at viakashmir.in/sign-up?role=vendor or write to contact@viakashmir.in.
