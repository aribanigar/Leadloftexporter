Kashmir is open and at its greenest this July. The Dal is glass-still at dawn, the Mughal gardens are in full bloom, and Gulmarg's meadows are carpeted with wildflowers with the gondola running. Flights into Srinagar are steady and the valley is planned end to end by people who live here - houseboats, hotels, cabs and shikaras all sorted. If Kashmir is on your list this year, this is the window. Plan your trip at viakashmir.in.
