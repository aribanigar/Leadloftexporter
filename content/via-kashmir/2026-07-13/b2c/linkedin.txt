While the plains cross forty degrees, Kashmir is waking up near fifteen. This mid-July the meadows are emerald, the Mughal gardens are in full bloom, and the Dal is glass-still at first light before the afternoon clouds roll in. Flights into Srinagar are steady and the whole valley is planned end to end by people who live here - houseboats, hotels, cabs and shikaras all sorted. If you want a cool green escape this summer, this is the window. Plan your trip at viakashmir.in.
