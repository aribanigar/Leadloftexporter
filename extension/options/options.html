Capture behavior

Autopilot runs at a human pace (~45–180s between actions), pauses when the tab is hidden, and aborts on captchas. Daily caps are enforced server-side from your workspace outreach settings.

When you open any LinkedIn profile, LeadCaptura automatically saves the lead and enriches their name, title, company, location, email, and phone — no clicking required.

When you save a lead from a search result, opens the profile in a background tab, reads the “Contact info” overlay, and updates the lead. The tab closes itself when done.

While you browse LinkedIn messages, LeadCaptura reads the conversation list (which LinkedIn loads automatically) and saves each contact as a lead. No extra requests are made — it only observes data LinkedIn already fetched.

After saving a lead, if their company website is known but email/phone is still missing, fetches the website’s Contact and About pages and extracts any visible email addresses or phone numbers. Requires permission to access all websites.

Auto-Apply — Application profile

Used to auto-fill the standard questions on LinkedIn Easy Apply forms (phone, location, years of experience, salary, etc.). Leave a field blank to skip it.

Privacy

This extension reads only the DOM of LinkedIn pages you're already viewing. It never calls LinkedIn's internal APIs, never spawns headless sessions, and never reads any other site. Scraped data is sent only to your configured backend URL.
