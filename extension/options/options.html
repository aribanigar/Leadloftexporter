Capture behaviour

Auto-Apply — Application profile

Your answers to the standard questions on LinkedIn Easy Apply forms. Fill what applies to you — leave a field blank to skip it. Anything the auto-apply can't answer from here is learned the first time you answer it, and reused automatically after that.

LeadCaptura remembers the answer you give to every job-application question and auto-fills the same question next time — its memory grows the more you apply. “Reset learned answers” clears that memory so it starts fresh (your profile above is kept).

🤖

Auto-Apply — AI answers AI

For questions the profile above can't answer, LeadCaptura asks an AI using your CV text.

Privacy

This extension reads only the DOM of LinkedIn pages you're already viewing. It never calls LinkedIn's internal APIs, never spawns headless sessions, and never reads any other site. Scraped data is sent only to your configured backend URL.
